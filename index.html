💡 JioDive Quick Controls

  • Physical Button / Gaze Dwell: Click while aiming, or enable ⌛ Dwell 1.2s for hands-free control. Click empty space to hide/show the menu.
  • Double Click: Instantly recenters the screen if your phone drifts (or press R / Gamepad X/Y).
  • Bluetooth / Gamepad / Keys: Supports Xbox/PS/VR remotes + Space, Arrows, M, H, and Esc.
  • Optics & Thermal Dimmer: Toggle calibrated JioDive lens distortion (k1=0.101, k2=0.098) and 60%–100% dimmer live in VR.
  • iOS Safari: Tap Share ➔ "Add to Home Screen" to run in true full screen without browser toolbars.
