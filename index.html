Controls & JioDive Tips Click · Dwell · Gamepad
  • Conductive Trigger & Dwell: Tap the JioDive button while aiming to click, or tap empty space to hide/show the remote. Enable Gaze Dwell for hands-free control.
  • Instant Recenter: Double-tap the headset trigger cleanly anytime to snap the theater straight ahead (or press R / Gamepad X/Y).
  • Gamepad & Keyboard: Supports Bluetooth controllers (A click, B menu, D-Pad seek) and Space, Arrows, M, H, Esc.
  • iOS Safari Fullscreen: Tap Share → Add to Home Screen and launch from your Home Screen icon to hide browser toolbars.
