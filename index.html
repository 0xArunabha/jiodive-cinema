Controls & JioDive Tips

  • JioDive Trigger & Dwell: Tap the headset conductive button while aiming to click, or tap empty space to hide/show the remote. Enable Gaze Dwell in Optics for hands-free control.
  • Instant Recenter: Double-tap cleanly anytime to snap the screen straight ahead (or press R / Gamepad X/Y).
  • Controllers & Keyboard: Works with Bluetooth gamepads (A click, B menu, D-Pad seek) and keys Space, Arrows, M, H, Esc.
  • Android & iOS PWA: Opens upright in portrait like a native app and automatically locks to landscape when entering VR.
