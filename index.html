💡 JioDive Quick Controls

  • Physical Button: Click while aiming at buttons to select. Click in empty space to hide/show the menu.
  • Double Click: Instantly recenters the screen if your phone drifts.
  • Drift/Bed Mode: Toggle "Bed Mode" anytime to watch lying down flat.
  • Exit VR: Click "🚪 Exit VR" on the in-VR floating panel or exit fullscreen to change movies.
  • iOS Safari: Tap Share ➔ "Add to Home Screen" to run in true full screen without browser toolbars.
